Get support

If you experience a problem, send us a support request. Please include what you were doing, what happened, your device model and your app version. Do not include passwords or confidential farm data.

Create a support request

Before reporting a problem

  1. Confirm that your device has an internet connection.
  2. Close and reopen ChemStore.
  3. Confirm that you are signed in with the correct organisation account.
  4. Record the exact error message, if one appears.
