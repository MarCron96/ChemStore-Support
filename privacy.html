Privacy Policy

ChemStore Farm Inventory · Effective 27 August 2026

ChemStore Farm Inventory helps authorised agricultural teams manage chemical inventory, purchase orders, deliveries and related operational records.

Information we process

The app may process account information such as a name, email address, organisation and assigned role, together with inventory, order, delivery and audit information entered by authorised users.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, operate ChemStore features, synchronise authorised records, maintain security and provide support. We do not sell personal information or use ChemStore data for third-party advertising.

Device permissions

If a feature requests access to the camera, photo library or location, iOS will ask for permission first. Access is limited to the feature described in the system prompt and can be changed in iOS Settings. The app does not intentionally access these resources without user action and permission.

Storage and service providers

ChemStore uses cloud infrastructure, including Google Firebase services, to authenticate users and store authorised application data. These providers process information according to their security and privacy commitments.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and company records while needed to provide ChemStore. Authorised members of a company can access its shared operational records.

You can request account deletion in Account settings. The app asks you to verify your password and confirm the request. A company owner must transfer ownership to another eligible administrator or explicitly confirm company closure. Account deletion removes your login, profile and preferences and removes or redacts account attribution in shared records. Shared company inventory and transaction records can remain for the company's continued use. Company closure is a separate action that deletes the company's records and removes members' access to that company; it is not a request to delete every member's login.

A minimal completion record is scheduled for expiry after seven days. Private database exports are configured for deletion after 30 days. Expiry is processed asynchronously, so physical deletion may occur later. Recovery safeguards prevent exports made before a completed account deletion from being used as eligible recovery points. Ordinary operational logs are configured for 30-day retention; certain Google Cloud audit logs are retained for 400 days. These logs are separate from live app records and database exports.

If you cannot access your account, email support.chemstore@gmail.com for help. Never send your password. Records you have exported or shared outside ChemStore are not remotely removed from recipients' devices by deleting your account.

Security and access

Access is restricted through authentication and role-based permissions. No internet-based system can be guaranteed completely secure, so users should protect their credentials and report suspected unauthorised access promptly.

Children

ChemStore is a business application and is not directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated as ChemStore changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions or account-deletion requests, email support.chemstore@gmail.com. Never send your password. Include only the information needed to explain your request.
